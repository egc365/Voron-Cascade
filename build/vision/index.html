Cascade Vision Rig

The Cascade has a fixed gantry and a moving table. The spindle only moves in X and Z, at one fixed depth, so every cut happens on the same narrow strip in space. You don't need to blanket the enclosure. Three sensors aimed at that strip, the tool, and the table cover it, and the machine's own axes do the precise measuring.

Three sensors, three jobs

Each camera was placed in the model and ray-traced against every part to count what it can actually see. A camera that only watches would miss things. Each one here covers what the others can't.

Accuracy reality check

Cameras watch. The machine measures.

Depth cameras give millimetres. Machined parts need hundredths. A D405 is rated ±2% at 50 cm. Even at 26 cm, that is about a millimetre of absolute error, which is fine for "is the vise where CAM thinks it is" and useless for "is this pocket 12.00 mm deep".

For real measurement, use the ballscrews you already have. A touch probe (already an optional BOM line, with its own X-endstop mount) or a laser line on the Z carriage swept by the Y table measures in machine coordinates. Then the twin compares measured to planned in the same frame.

The twin loop

flowchart LR
  G[G-code job] --> F[FlexiHAL / grblHAL]
  F -- position + state --> T[Digital twin
on the Spark]
  G --> T
  T -- predicted stock --> D{Compare}
  CA[Cam A + B
live video] --> V[Vision model
on the Spark]
  CC[Cam C depth
pre-job scan] --> D
  S[Spindle load
vibration] --> V
  V --> D
  D -- anomaly --> H[Feed hold]
  H --> F
  P[Probe / laser scan] -- measured part --> R[Deviation report]
  T --> R
  1. Before the job, cam C scans the table. The twin checks that the stock and clamps match the CAM setup and stops a collision before the first move.
  2. During the job, the twin replays machine position and removes material from a virtual stock. Cams A and B, spindle load and vibration go to a vision model on the Spark.
  3. On an anomaly (tool snapped, chips packing, part shifting, smoke), the Spark sends a feed hold back to grblHAL.
  4. After the job, the probe or laser-line scan measures the part in machine coordinates. The twin reports deviation from the model.

Default config: AC spindle (G-Penny 1.5 kW + H100 VFD), FlexiHAL with DM542T drivers, T-slot table, and the M8 80×30 Z plate. That plate matches the BOM's 65 mm clamp with the 80×30 bolt pattern. Change any of these in build/config.json and the lists regenerate.
